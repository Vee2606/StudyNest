Overview

Algebra is a way of using letters to represent numbers or values we do not know yet. Instead of writing long sentences, Algebra helps us write mathematical ideas in a shorter and clearer way.

StudyNest approach: We will not rush. Each lesson explains the idea first, then shows examples, then gives you a chance to practise.

🎯 Learning Objectives

By completing the Algebra learning path, you should be able to:

  • Understand why letters are used in Mathematics
  • Identify variables, constants, coefficients and terms
  • Write and simplify algebraic expressions
  • Collect like terms correctly
  • Substitute values into expressions and formulae
  • Expand single and double brackets
  • Factorise linear and simple quadratic expressions
  • Solve linear equations and word problems
  • Change the subject of a formula
  • Solve simultaneous equations using elimination and substitution
  • Solve and interpret inequalities
  • Recognise, expand and factorise quadratic expressions
  • Solve simple quadratic equations by factorisation
  • Simplify and calculate with algebraic fractions
  • Choose suitable methods and check your answers
