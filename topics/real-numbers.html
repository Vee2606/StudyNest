📖 Complete Revision Guide

This section will become the full Real Numbers revision pack.

1. Number Types

Number types help us classify numbers correctly. Examples include natural numbers, whole numbers, integers, even numbers, odd numbers, prime numbers and composite numbers.

2. Factors and Multiples

Factors divide into a number exactly. Multiples are the answers you get when multiplying a number.

3. Directed Numbers

Directed numbers include positive and negative numbers. They are useful for temperatures, debts, bank balances and positions on a number line.
