INDICES & LOGARITHMS · LESSON 02

Laws of Indices

Use the multiplication, division and power laws correctly.

⏱️ 25–40 min 📘 Core

🎯 Learning Objectives

  • multiply and divide powers with the same base;
  • raise a power to another power;
  • apply powers to products and quotients.

The Three Main Laws

Multiplication

a m a n =a m+n

Add the indices when the base is the same.

Division

a m ÷a n =a m−n

Subtract the indices when the base is the same.

Power of a power

(a m ) n =a mn

Multiply the indices.

Exam tip: The bases must be the same before you add or subtract indices.

✏️ Why does the multiplication law work?

a 3 ×a 2 =(a×a×a)(a×a)=a 5

There are five factors of a , so 3+2=5 .

🧩 Law Builder

Common mistake: a 3 +a 3 =2a 3 , not a 6 . The multiplication law does not apply to addition.
