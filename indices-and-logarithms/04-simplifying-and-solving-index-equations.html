INDICES & LOGARITHMS · LESSON 04

Simplifying & Index Equations

Simplify expressions and solve equations by matching bases.

⏱️ 25–40 min 📘 Challenge

🎯 Learning Objectives

  • simplify expressions involving several index laws;
  • write answers with positive indices;
  • solve index equations by expressing both sides with the same base.

✏️ Simplifying Expressions

Simplify (3x 4 y −2 )(2x 3 y 5 ) .

Multiply coefficients: 3×2=6 .
Add indices of x : x 4+3 =x 7 .
Add indices of y : y −2+5 =y 3 .
Answer: 6x 7 y 3 .

Solving Index Equations

When possible, rewrite both sides using the same base. Then equate the indices.

2 x+1 =16=2 4 ⇒ x+1=4 ⇒ x=3

Step 1

Express both sides with the same base.

Step 2

Equate indices because equal powers of the same positive base have equal exponents.

Step 3

Solve the resulting linear or quadratic equation.

✏️ A harder example

Solve 9 x =27 .

Use base 3: 9=3 2 and 27=3 3 .
(3 2 ) x =3 3 , so 3 2x =3 3 .
2x=3 , hence x=3/2 .

Practice

  1. Solve 5 x =125 .
  2. Solve 2 2x−1 =32 .
  3. Solve 27 x =9 .
  4. Simplify (a 5 b −2 )/(a 2 b 3 ) using positive indices.
Show answers
  1. x=3
  2. x=3
  3. x=2/3
  4. a 3 /b 5
