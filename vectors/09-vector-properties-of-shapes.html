Vector Properties of Plane Shapes

Use routes, diagonals and scalar multiples to reason about shapes.

⏱️ 24–34 min 📘 Challenge➶ Diagrams included
a⃗ b⃗ |v|

🎯 Learning Objectives

  • express edges and diagonals using vectors;
  • use parallelogram and triangle properties;
  • simplify vector routes;
  • prove lines are parallel or points are collinear.

Vectors in a Parallelogram

Proving Parallelism

If one vector is a scalar multiple of another, the lines are parallel.

🧭 Interactive Diagram: Parallelogram Vector Properties

Drag the orange vertex. Opposite sides remain equal and parallel, and the diagonal is the sum of two adjacent side vectors.

Try it: drag vertex C and observe how AB = DC and AD = BC remain true.

AB and DC are equal; AD and BC are equal; AC = AB + BC.

Practice

  1. In parallelogram ABCD, AB⃗=a and AD⃗=b. Express AC⃗.
  2. Express DB⃗ in terms of a and b.
  3. Explain why vectors (4,6) and (−2,−3) are parallel.
  4. What vector condition shows three points are collinear?

✅ Check Your Work

Show answers and working
  1. AC⃗=a+b.
  2. DB⃗=DA⃗+AB⃗=−b+a=a−b.
  3. (4,6)=−2(−2,−3), so they are scalar multiples.
  4. Two direction vectors along the same line are scalar multiples.

🧭 StudyNest: How to Solve Vector Problems

1ReadIdentify the starting point, endpoint, direction and required vector.
2RepresentDraw an arrow or write the vector in column form.
3ChooseDecide whether to add, subtract, scale or find magnitude.
4WorkOperate on horizontal and vertical components separately.
5SimplifyCollect like vector terms and reduce numerical components.
6InterpretState what the result means as a movement or displacement.
7CheckConfirm the direction and signs match the diagram.
8ConcludeGive the final vector or magnitude in correct notation.

📘 Remember

Vectors carry both magnitude and direction. Work with horizontal and vertical components separately, and keep signs consistent.

⚠️ Common Mistakes

Do not confuse a point with its position vector. For AB, always calculate B − A, not A − B.

⭐ Exam Tips

Sketch arrows, label directions clearly, show component working and check whether the final direction agrees with the diagram.

🌍 Did You Know?

Vectors are used in navigation, animation, engineering, robotics, weather modelling and computer games.
