PROBABILITY · LESSON 11

Venn-Diagram Probability

Use set regions to calculate unions, intersections and complements.

⏱️ 25–37 min 📘 Developing 🎲 Visual models included
½ 50% P(A)
H

🎯 Learning Objectives

  • read probability information from Venn diagrams;
  • calculate unions, intersections and complements;
  • convert frequencies into probabilities;
  • connect set notation to probability language.

Set and Probability Language

A∩B

A and B both occur.

A∪B

A or B or both occur.

A′

A does not occur.

Outside both

Neither A nor B occurs.

Worked Example

In a group of 40 learners: 18 study French, 15 study Shona and 6 study both.

French only=18−6=12.

Shona only=15−6=9.

Union=12+6+9=27.

P(F∪S)=27/40.

Neither=40−27=13, so P(neither)=13/40.

Practice

  1. Using the example, find P(F∩S).
  2. Find P(F only).
  3. Find P(not F).
  4. Verify the union using 18+15−6.

✅ Check Your Work

Show answers and working
  1. 6/40=3/20.
  2. 12/40=3/10.
  3. Not F includes Shona-only and neither: (9+13)/40=22/40=11/20.
  4. 18+15−6=27, matching the region total.

🧭 StudyNest: How to Solve Probability Problems

1 Describe State the experiment, event and exact outcome required.
2 List Write the sample space or organise outcomes in a table or tree.
3 Check Decide whether outcomes are equally likely and whether replacement occurs.
4 Choose Select complement, addition, multiplication or experimental probability.
5 Calculate Count favourable outcomes or follow and combine branches.
6 Simplify Give the probability as a fraction, decimal or percentage as requested.
7 Interpret Explain what the probability says about repeated trials.
8 Check Ensure the answer lies between 0 and 1 and branch totals are correct.

Lesson Support

💡 Remember

Venn diagrams show sets, overlaps and the region outside the sets.

⚠️ Common Mistakes

Do not count the intersection twice when finding a union.

⭐ Exam Tips

Fill the intersection first, then the non-overlapping regions.
