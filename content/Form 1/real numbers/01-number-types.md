# Number Types

## Why are we learning this?

Number types help us classify numbers correctly.

## Learning Objectives

- Identify natural numbers
- Identify whole numbers
- Identify integers
- Identify prime numbers

## Notes

Natural numbers are counting numbers.

Whole numbers include zero.

Integers include negative numbers.

## Worked Example

Is 7 prime?

Yes.

Its only factors are 1 and 7.

## Practice

1. List five natural numbers.
2. Is 9 prime?
3. Which are integers?

## Summary

Knowing number types helps us understand every other maths topic.
