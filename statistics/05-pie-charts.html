STATISTICS · LESSON 05

Pie Charts

Convert frequencies into sector angles and interpret proportional data.

⏱️ 22–30 min 📘 Developing 📊 Interpretation included
x̄ % Σ

🎯 Learning Objectives

  • calculate sector angles;
  • construct pie charts;
  • recover frequencies from angles;
  • compare proportions;
  • check that sectors total 360°.

Central Relationship

Sector angle = frequency ÷ total frequency × 360°
Frequency = sector angle ÷ 360° × total frequency

Worked Example

In a survey of 40 learners, 10 choose football. Sector angle=10÷40×360°=90°.

Thinking Check

Before calculating, estimate: 10 out of 40 is one quarter, so the sector should be one quarter of a circle.

Practice

  1. Find the sector angle for 15 out of 60 learners.
  2. A sector is 72° in a survey of 50 people. Find the frequency.
  3. Frequencies are 8, 12 and 20. Find all sector angles.
  4. How can you check a completed pie chart?

✅ Check Your Work

Show answers and working
  1. 15÷60×360=90°.
  2. 72÷360×50=10 people.
  3. Total=40. Angles: 72°, 108° and 180°.
  4. Add all sector angles; they must total 360°. Also compare sector sizes with the original proportions.

Summary

  • Pie charts show proportions of a whole.
  • Angles must total 360°.
