Introduction to Statistics

Understand what statistics is, why it matters and how data can be classified.

  • choose suitable ways to collect information.

What Is Statistics?

Statistics is the process of collecting, organising, presenting, analysing and interpreting data so that we can make informed decisions.

Data becomes useful only when we understand what it represents and how it was collected.

Types of Data

Qualitative

Descriptions or categories, such as eye colour or favourite sport.

Quantitative

Numerical values, such as height, age or test score.

Discrete

Counted values, usually whole numbers, such as number of siblings.

Continuous

Measured values that may take any value in an interval, such as mass or time.

Primary and Secondary Data

Primary

Collected first-hand for the current investigation.

Secondary

Already collected by another person or organisation.
