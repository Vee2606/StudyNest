Mixed Questions

  1. Classify height as discrete or continuous.
  2. Explain one advantage and one risk of using a sample.
  3. Find the sector angle for frequency 18 out of 72.
  4. Find mean, median and mode of 2,3,3,4,8.
  5. Find the range of 11,14,20,22,31.
  6. Find density for frequency 45 and width 15.
  7. Find the midpoint of 60≤x<80.
  8. For N=120, state the median and quartile positions.
  9. Explain why an estimated grouped mean is not exact.
  10. State one reason a graph may mislead.

  1. Continuous.
  2. Advantage: faster/cheaper. Risk: an unrepresentative sample gives biased results.
  3. 18÷72×360=90°.
  4. Mean=20÷5=4; median=3; mode=3.
  5. 31−11=20.
  6. 45÷15=3.
  7. 70.
  8. Q₁=30th, median=60th, Q₃=90th.
  9. Class midpoints are used because individual values are unknown.
  10. For example, a truncated axis, unequal pictogram symbols, missing labels or inappropriate scale.
