Grouped Data and Estimated Mean

Use class midpoints to estimate averages from grouped frequency tables.

⏱️ 22–30 min 📘 Developing 📊 Interpretation included
x̄ % Σ

🎯 Learning Objectives

  • find class midpoints;
  • estimate the mean of grouped data;
  • identify the modal class;
  • understand why grouped means are estimates;
  • use an assumed mean efficiently.

Estimated Mean

Estimated mean = Σ(f×midpoint) ÷ Σf
ClassfMidpoint xfx
0≤t<103515
10≤t<2051575
20≤t<3022550

Estimated mean=(15+75+50)÷10=14.

The exact values inside each class are unknown, so the midpoint calculation gives an estimate.

Assumed Mean Idea

Choose a convenient midpoint A and calculate deviations d=x−A. Then use A+Σfd÷Σf.

Practice

✅ Check Your Work

Show answers and working
  1. (30+40)÷2=35.
  2. 10≤t<20, because it has the highest frequency 5.
  3. The individual values are not known; every value in a class is represented by the midpoint.
  4. Σfx=140 and Σf=10, so mean=140÷10=14.

Summary

  • Midpoints stand in for unknown values.
  • Always divide Σfx by Σf, not by the number of classes.
