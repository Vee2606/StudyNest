🟡 Form 2 Mathematics

Build on Form 1 foundations with deeper work in numbers, algebra, geometry, graphs, statistics and variation.

📘 Real Numbers

Squares, square roots, cubes, cube roots, order of operations, ratios and proportions.

📗 Algebra

Substitution, algebraic expressions, algebraic fractions, factorisation and equations.

📐 Geometry

Angles, parallel lines, polygons, similarity and constructions.

📊 Statistics

Frequency tables, bar charts, pie charts, mean, median and mode.

📈 Graphs

Linear graphs, tables of values and distance-time graphs.

🔁 Variation

Direct variation and inverse variation.
