🟠 Form 3 Mathematics

Move into more advanced O Level concepts including standard form, quadratic work, matrices, vectors and trigonometry.

📘 Real Numbers

Irrational numbers, number patterns, standard form, number bases and rates.

📗 Algebra

Algebraic fractions, quadratic expressions, simultaneous equations and inequalities.

📈 Graphs

Functional notation, linear graphs, quadratic graphs and travel graphs.

📐 Geometry

Polygons, bearings, loci, similarity, congruency and symmetry.

📊 Statistics

Grouped data, histograms, frequency polygons and measures of central tendency.

🧮 Matrices

Order, types, operations, determinants and inverse matrices.

➡️ Vectors

Vector notation, types of vectors, addition, subtraction and magnitude.

🔄 Transformations

Translation, reflection, rotation and enlargement.

📐 Trigonometry

Pythagoras and trigonometrical ratios.
