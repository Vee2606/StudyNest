TRANSFORMATIONS · LESSON 10

Shears

Slant figures using a shear factor and invariant line.

⏱️ 26–38 min 📘 Challenge 📍 Coordinate diagrams included
T ↻ k=2

🎯 Learning Objectives

  • describe horizontal and vertical shears;
  • identify shear factor and invariant line;
  • apply shear matrices;
  • recognise how shapes slant while preserving area.

Shear Matrices

Horizontal shear factor k

1k01; x′=x+ky.

Vertical shear factor k

10k1; y′=kx+y.

Invariant Lines

For a horizontal shear, the x-axis is invariant. For a vertical shear, the y-axis is invariant.

Worked Example

Horizontal shear factor 2 applied to (3,4):

x′=3+2(4)=11, y′=4, so image=(11,4).

Practice

  1. Apply horizontal shear factor 1 to (2,5).
  2. Apply vertical shear factor −2 to (3,4).
  3. State the invariant line for each.

✅ Check Your Work

Show answers and working
  1. x′=2+1(5)=7, y′=5, so (7,5).
  2. x′=3; y′=−2(3)+4=−2, so (3,−2).
  3. Horizontal shear: x-axis. Vertical shear: y-axis.

🧠 Learn It Visually: Shears

A shear slants a figure. One coordinate stays fixed while the other changes by an amount proportional to it.

Invariant axis

A standard shear leaves one axis fixed.

Parallel sliding

Rows or columns of points slide parallel to the invariant axis.

Shape change

Area is preserved for standard unit shears, but angles and lengths generally change.

🧷 Remember

In an x-shear, y-values stay unchanged; in a y-shear, x-values stay unchanged.

⚠️ Common Mistake

Assuming a shear preserves angles because it preserves area.

🎯 Exam Tip

Check which coordinate remains unchanged to identify the invariant axis.

🧭 StudyNest: How to Solve Transformation Problems

1 Compare Look at the object and image before calculating anything.
2 Identify Decide whether the change is a slide, flip, turn, resize, stretch or shear.
3 Locate Find the vector, mirror line, centre, invariant line or centre of enlargement.
4 Measure Determine direction, angle and scale factor where needed.
5 Map Apply the coordinate rule or matrix to one vertex at a time.
6 Plot Plot every image point accurately and join them in the same order.
7 Describe Name the transformation completely—not only its type.
8 Check Verify distances, orientation, size and invariant features.
