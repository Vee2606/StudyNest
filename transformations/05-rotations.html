TRANSFORMATIONS · LESSON 05

Rotations

Turn figures about a centre and describe the rotation completely.

⏱️ 26–38 min 📘 Developing 📍 Coordinate diagrams included
T ↻ k=2

🎯 Learning Objectives

  • rotate figures about the origin and other centres;
  • state centre, angle and direction;
  • use coordinate rules for multiples of 90°;
  • find a centre of rotation geometrically.

Complete Description

Rotation = centre + angle + direction.

90° anticlockwise about origin

(x,y)→(−y,x)

90° clockwise about origin

(x,y)→(y,−x)

180° about origin

(x,y)→(−x,−y)

Finding the Centre

Join two object points to their images. Construct perpendicular bisectors. Their intersection is the centre of rotation.

Practice

  1. Rotate (3,2) 90° anticlockwise about the origin.
  2. Rotate (−4,1) 90° clockwise about the origin.
  3. Rotate (5,−3) 180° about the origin.
  4. Why is “rotation of 90°” incomplete?

✅ Check Your Work

Show answers and working
  1. (−2,3).
  2. (1,4).
  3. (−5,3).
  4. It omits the centre and direction.

🧠 Learn It Visually: Rotations

A rotation is a turn about a fixed point called the centre of rotation. Every point travels along a circular arc.

Centre

The one fixed point around which the figure turns.

Angle

State the size of the turn, usually 90°, 180° or 270°.

Direction

Clockwise and anticlockwise give different images unless the angle is 180°.

🧷 Remember

A complete rotation description includes centre, angle and direction.

⚠️ Common Mistake

Rotating around the origin when the stated centre is elsewhere.

🎯 Exam Tip

Trace from the centre to one vertex, turn that radius, then repeat for every vertex.

🧭 StudyNest: How to Solve Transformation Problems

1 Compare Look at the object and image before calculating anything.
2 Identify Decide whether the change is a slide, flip, turn, resize, stretch or shear.
3 Locate Find the vector, mirror line, centre, invariant line or centre of enlargement.
4 Measure Determine direction, angle and scale factor where needed.
5 Map Apply the coordinate rule or matrix to one vertex at a time.
6 Plot Plot every image point accurately and join them in the same order.
7 Describe Name the transformation completely—not only its type.
8 Check Verify distances, orientation, size and invariant features.
