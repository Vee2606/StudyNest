TRANSFORMATIONS · LESSON 06

Enlargements

Resize figures using a centre and rational scale factor.

⏱️ 26–38 min 📘 Developing 📍 Coordinate diagrams included
T ↻ k=2

🎯 Learning Objectives

  • enlarge figures from the origin or another centre;
  • use positive, fractional and negative scale factors;
  • find scale factor and centre;
  • describe enlargement completely.

Complete Description

Enlargement = centre of enlargement + scale factor.

k>1

Image is larger and on the same rays.

0<k<1

Image is smaller and on the same rays.

k<0

Image lies on the opposite side of the centre.

Coordinates About the Origin

(x,y)→(kx,ky).

For k=2, (3,−1)→(6,−2).

Finding the Centre

Join corresponding vertices and extend the lines. Their common intersection is the centre of enlargement.

Practice

  1. Enlarge (2,−3) by scale factor 3 about the origin.
  2. Enlarge (8,4) by scale factor 1/2 about the origin.
  3. What does scale factor −2 mean?
  4. An object side is 4 cm and image side is 10 cm. Find k.

✅ Check Your Work

Show answers and working
  1. (6,−9).
  2. (4,2).
  3. Twice the size, with corresponding points on the opposite side of the centre.
  4. k=10/4=2.5.

🧠 Learn It Visually: Enlargements

An enlargement changes every distance from a fixed centre by the same multiplier called the scale factor. It may make a figure larger, smaller or place it on the opposite side of the centre.

Centre of enlargement

Object point, centre and image point lie on one straight ray.

Scale factor k

Image distance from centre = |k| × object distance.

Negative or fractional k

A fraction shrinks. A negative factor places the image on the opposite side and reverses direction through the centre.

🧷 Remember

Lengths multiply by |k|, areas multiply by k², and angles stay unchanged.

⚠️ Common Mistake

Measuring scale factor from the origin instead of from the stated centre.

🎯 Exam Tip

To find the centre, join at least two pairs of corresponding vertices and extend the lines until they meet.

🧭 StudyNest: How to Solve Transformation Problems

1 Compare Look at the object and image before calculating anything.
2 Identify Decide whether the change is a slide, flip, turn, resize, stretch or shear.
3 Locate Find the vector, mirror line, centre, invariant line or centre of enlargement.
4 Measure Determine direction, angle and scale factor where needed.
5 Map Apply the coordinate rule or matrix to one vertex at a time.
6 Plot Plot every image point accurately and join them in the same order.
7 Describe Name the transformation completely—not only its type.
8 Check Verify distances, orientation, size and invariant features.
