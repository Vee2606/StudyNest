Linear Equations

A linear equation is a mathematical statement showing that two expressions are equal. Your goal is to find the value of the unknown variable.

🤔 Think About This...

Imagine you have a balance scale. Everything on the left weighs exactly the same as everything on the right.

⚖️

If you remove one book from the left side, what must you do on the right side?

Exactly! You must remove one book from the right side too. Otherwise the scale will no longer balance.

An equation works exactly like a balance scale. Whatever you do to one side, you must also do to the other side.

📖 What is an Equation?

An equation is a mathematical sentence showing that two expressions have the same value.

x + 5 = 12

The equals sign (=) means "is equal to." It does not mean "the answer comes next." It tells us that both sides have the same value.

Think of "=" as saying "the left side balances the right side."

🧠 The StudyNest Thinking Method

Whenever you solve an equation, ask yourself:

👉 What am I trying to leave on its own?

The answer is always:

The variable

Everything you do should help isolate the variable.

🔑 Inverse Operations

To isolate the variable, we use the opposite operation.

Operation Opposite Operation
Add Subtract
Subtract Add
Multiply Divide
Divide Multiply
Think of inverse operations as "undo" buttons.

📝 Worked Examples

Example 1

Solve:

x + 5 = 12

🔍 StudyNest Question: What are we trying to leave on its own?

We want to leave x by itself.

5 is being added. Undo it by subtracting 5 from BOTH sides.

x + 5 − 5 = 12 − 5
x = 7
Answer: x = 7

Example 2

Solve:

x − 8 = 15

Undo subtraction by adding 8 to both sides.

x − 8 + 8 = 15 + 8
x = 23
Answer: x = 23

Example 3

Solve:

4x = 28

🔍 StudyNest Question: What operation is being done to x?

x is being multiplied by 4. Undo multiplication by dividing both sides by 4.

4x ÷ 4 = 28 ÷ 4
x = 7
Answer: x = 7

Example 4

Solve:

x ÷ 5 = 6

x is being divided by 5. Undo division by multiplying both sides by 5.

(x ÷ 5) × 5 = 6 × 5
x = 30
Answer: x = 30

Example 5

Solve:

3x + 4 = 19

🔍 StudyNest Question: What must we undo first?

We undo the addition first. Subtract 4 from both sides.

3x + 4 − 4 = 19 − 4
3x = 15

Now divide both sides by 3.

3x ÷ 3 = 15 ÷ 3
x = 5
Answer: x = 5

Example 6

Solve:

5x − 7 = 18

Add 7 to both sides.

5x − 7 + 7 = 18 + 7
5x = 25

Divide both sides by 5.

x = 5
Answer: x = 5

🪜 The StudyNest Equation Shortcut

Undo → Balance → Simplify
  1. Undo: Use the opposite operation.
  2. Balance: Do the same thing to both sides.
  3. Simplify: Continue until the variable is alone.

🕵️ Check Your Answer

A good maths detective always checks the evidence.

Suppose we solved:

x + 5 = 12

and found:

x = 7

Substitute 7 back into the original equation:

7 + 5 = 12
12 = 12, so the answer is correct.

🌍 Real-Life Example

A student buys a notebook and a pen.

The pen costs $2, and the total cost is $8.

Let the notebook cost be x.

x + 2 = 8

Subtract 2 from both sides.

x = 6
The notebook costs $6.

⚠ Common Mistakes

Mistake 1:

Doing something to only one side of the equation.

An equation must remain balanced.
Mistake 2:

Changing signs without showing the operation.

It is safer to write the same operation on both sides.
Mistake 3:

Dividing only one term instead of the entire side.
Mistake 4:

Stopping before the variable is completely alone.
