🧠 StudyNest Question Strategy

Before solving any Algebra question, ask:

  1. What information has been given?
  2. What am I being asked to find?
  3. Which Algebra skill does this question test?
  4. What method should I use?
  5. Can I check my answer?

Section A — Algebra Foundations

Simplify or answer each question.

  1. In the expression 7x + 4, identify:

    a) the variable
    b) the coefficient
    c) the constant
  2. How many terms are in:
    5x² − 3x + 8
  3. Write an algebraic expression for:
    A number x increased by 9
  4. Write an algebraic expression for:
    Five times a number y, then subtract 4
  5. Simplify:
    4x + 7x
  6. Simplify:
    9a − 3a + 5
  7. Simplify:
    6x + 4y − 2x + 3y
  8. Simplify:
    3x² + 5x − x² + 2x
  9. State whether the terms 5x and 5x² are like terms. Give a reason.
  10. If x = 4, evaluate:
    3x + 7

Section B — Substitution and Formulae

  1. Evaluate:
    2x² − 3x + 1
    when x = 3.
  2. Evaluate:
    4a − 2b
    when a = 5 and b = −3.
  3. The perimeter of a rectangle is given by:
    P = 2l + 2w
    Find P when l = 8 cm and w = 5 cm.
  4. The distance travelled is given by:
    d = vt
    Find d when v = 12 m/s and t = 7 seconds.
  5. Use:
    A = ½bh
    to calculate the area of a triangle with base 10 cm and height 7 cm.
  6. Make x the subject:
    y = x + 6
  7. Make a the subject:
    b = 3a
  8. Make r the subject:
    C = 2πr
  9. Make h the subject:
    A = ½bh
  10. Make x the subject:
    y = 4x − 7

Section D — Linear Equations and Word Problems

  1. Solve:
    x + 8 = 17
  2. Solve:
    5x = 35
  3. Solve:
    3x + 4 = 19
  4. Solve:
    4x − 7 = 2x + 9
  5. Solve:
    3(x + 2) = 21
  6. Solve:
    2(x − 3) + 5 = 13
  7. A number is increased by 7. The result is 19. Form an equation and find the number.
  8. Three times a number, decreased by 4, is equal to 20. Form an equation and find the number.
  9. The length of a rectangle is 3 cm greater than its width. The perimeter is 30 cm. Find the length and width.
  10. Tariro buys 4 identical exercise books and pays $12. Write and solve an equation to find the cost of one exercise book.

Section E — Simultaneous Equations and Inequalities

  1. Solve:
    x + y = 8
    x − y = 2
  2. Solve:
    2x + y = 11
    x − y = 1
  3. Solve:
    x + y = 9
    y = x + 3
  4. Solve:
    3x + 2y = 16
    x + y = 6
  5. Solve:
    4x − 5 > 15
  6. Solve:
    2x + 3 ≤ 11
  7. Solve:
    −3x > 12
  8. Represent:
    x ≥ 4
    on a number line.
  9. Represent:
    −2 < x ≤ 5
    on a number line.

Section F — Quadratic Equations

  1. Factorise:
    x² + 8x + 15
  2. Factorise:
    x² − 5x − 14
  3. Solve by factorisation:
    x² + 5x + 6 = 0
  4. Solve by factorisation:
    x² − 9x + 20 = 0
  5. Solve:
    2x² + 10x = 0
  6. Solve by completing the square:
    x² + 6x − 7 = 0
  7. Solve using the quadratic formula:
    2x² + 3x − 2 = 0
  8. Solve using the quadratic formula:
    x² − 4x − 1 = 0
  9. State the discriminant of:
    x² − 6x + 9 = 0
    What does it tell you?
  10. State the discriminant of:
    x² + 2x + 5 = 0
    What does it tell you?
