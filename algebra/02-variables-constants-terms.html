Variables, Constants & Terms

Now that you know Algebra uses letters, let’s learn the names of the parts we see in algebraic expressions.

🎯 By the end of this lesson...

You should be able to:

  • Explain what a variable is.
  • Explain what a constant is.
  • Identify terms in an expression.
  • Identify coefficients in simple algebraic expressions.

🤔 Let’s start with a simple idea

Imagine you are buying exercise books.

You do not know the price of one exercise book yet, so we call the price:

x

If you buy 3 exercise books, the cost is:

3x

In this expression, the letter and the number each have a special name. Learning these names helps you understand Algebra more clearly.

🔤 What is a Variable?

A variable is a letter that represents a number we do not know yet, or a number that can change.

Examples of variables: x, y, a, n, p

In Algebra, a letter is not there to confuse you. It simply stands for a value.

💡 Think of a variable as an empty box waiting for a number.

🔢 What is a Constant?

A constant is a number that does not change.

Examples of constants: 2, 5, 10, 25, 100

In the expression below:

x + 5

x is the variable, because it can change. 5 is the constant, because it stays fixed.

📦 What is a Term?

A term is a single part of an algebraic expression. Terms are separated by plus or minus signs.

Look at this expression:

3x + 5

This expression has two terms:

  • 3x
  • 5
Terms are like separate pieces of a sentence in Algebra.

✖️ What is a Coefficient?

A coefficient is the number in front of a variable. It tells us how many of that variable we have.

4x

In 4x, the coefficient is 4. It means we have 4 groups of x.

7y

In 7y, the coefficient is 7.

🌍 Real-Life Example

Suppose one kombi trip costs x dollars.

If you take 5 trips, the total cost is:

5x
  • x is the variable.
  • 5 is the coefficient.
  • 5x is one term.

📝 Worked Examples

Example 1

Identify the variable and constant in:

x + 8

Thinking...

The letter is the variable. The fixed number is the constant.

Variable: x
Constant: 8

Example 2

Identify the coefficient in:

6y

Thinking...

The coefficient is the number in front of the variable.

Coefficient: 6

Example 3

How many terms are in:

2x + 3

Thinking...

The plus sign separates the expression into parts.

There are 2 terms: 2x and 3
