Substitution

Substitution means replacing a letter with its given value. Once the letter has been replaced, the expression becomes an ordinary calculation.

🎬 Replace the letter with its value

2x + 3
→
2(4) + 3

For x = 4, substitute first, then calculate.

🤔 Think About This...

Imagine that one bottle of juice costs x dollars.

The cost of three bottles can be written as:

3x

Now suppose the shop tells you that one bottle costs $4.

This means:

x = 4

We replace x with 4:

3 × 4 = 12
Three bottles cost $12.

You have just used substitution.

📖 What is Substitution?

Substitution means replacing a variable with its given value.

For example, if:

x = 6

Find the value of:

x + 5

Replace x with 6:

6 + 5
x + 5 = 11

🪜 The StudyNest Substitution Shortcut

Substitute → Simplify → Solve
  1. Substitute: Replace every letter with its given value.
  2. Simplify: Rewrite the expression as a calculation.
  3. Solve: Work out the final answer.
Do not try to calculate before replacing the variable.

✖️ Remember Hidden Multiplication

When a number is written next to a variable, multiplication is understood.

5x means 5 × x

So if:

x = 4

Then:

5x = 5 × 4
5x = 20
Do not join the numbers together. If x = 4, then 5x is not 54.

📝 Worked Examples

Example 1

If x = 9, find:

x − 4

Step 1: Substitute

9 − 4

Step 2: Solve

9 − 4 = 5

Example 2

If a = 7, find:

3a

Step 1: Substitute

3 × 7

Step 2: Solve

3a = 21

Example 3

If m = 5, find:

2m + 8

Step 1: Substitute

2(5) + 8

Step 2: Simplify

10 + 8

Step 3: Solve

2m + 8 = 18

Example 4

If x = 6 and y = 3, find:

x + y

Step 1: Substitute both values

6 + 3

Step 2: Solve

x + y = 9

Example 5

If a = 4 and b = 2, find:

3a − b

Step 1: Substitute

3(4) − 2

Step 2: Simplify

12 − 2

Step 3: Solve

3a − b = 10

➖ Substituting Negative Numbers

Negative numbers should be placed inside brackets when substituted.

If:

x = −3

Find:

4x

Substitute using brackets:

4(−3)
4x = −12
💡 Brackets help you keep the negative sign attached to the number.

🌍 Algebra Around Us

🍞 Bread

If one loaf costs b dollars and b = 2, then five loaves cost:

5b = 5 × 2 = 10

🚌 Kombi Fare

If one trip costs k dollars and k = 1.50, then four trips cost:

4k = 4 × 1.50 = 6

📱 Airtime

If x = 5, then:

2x + 10 = 2(5) + 10 = 20

📚 Exercise Books

If one book costs p dollars and p = 3, then six books cost:

6p = 6 × 3 = 18

⚠ Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Joining numbers instead of multiplying.

If x = 4, then 5x = 5 × 4 = 20, not 54.
Mistake 2: Replacing only one occurrence of the variable.

If x = 3, then x + 2x becomes 3 + 2(3).
Mistake 3: Losing a negative sign.

Use brackets when substituting negative values.
