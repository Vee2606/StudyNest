Algebraic Expressions

Now that you understand variables, constants and terms, let’s learn how they come together to form algebraic expressions.

🎯 By the end of this lesson...

You should be able to:

  • Explain what an algebraic expression is.
  • Write algebraic expressions from words.
  • Identify parts of an expression.
  • Tell the difference between an expression and an equation.

🤔 Think About This...

Imagine you walk into a supermarket and buy:

  • 3 bottles of Mazoe
  • 2 loaves of bread

You do not know the price of either item.

Instead of writing:

three times the price of Mazoe plus two times the price of bread

Mathematicians can simply write:

3m + 2b

Here, m represents the price of one bottle of Mazoe, and b represents the price of one loaf of bread.

💡 This is an algebraic expression. It is a shorter way of writing a mathematical idea.

📖 What is an Algebraic Expression?

An algebraic expression is a mathematical phrase made up of numbers, variables and operation signs.

Examples of operation signs include:

+   −   ×   ÷

Examples of algebraic expressions are:

x + 5
3a
2x + 7
4m + 2b
An algebraic expression does not have an equals sign.

⭐ Expression or Equation?

This is very important.

📌 Expression

An expression describes something.

3x + 5

No equals sign.

📌 Equation

An equation says two things are equal.

3x + 5 = 20

It has an equals sign.

🎯 Remember: Expression = no equals sign. Equation = has an equals sign.

🌍 Real-Life Examples

🍞 Bread

If one loaf costs b, then 5 loaves cost:

5b

🚌 Kombi Trips

If one trip costs k, then 4 trips cost:

4k

📱 Airtime

If one airtime voucher costs a, then 2 vouchers plus $5 cost:

2a + 5

⚽ Football

If one ticket costs t and one drink costs d, then 3 tickets and 2 drinks cost:

3t + 2d

📝 Worked Examples

Example 1

Write an expression for:

Five more than a number

Thinking...

The unknown number is x. Five more means add 5.

x + 5

Example 2

Write an expression for:

Seven times a number

Thinking...

The unknown number is n. Seven times means multiply by 7.

7n

Example 3

Write an expression for:

Three less than a number

Thinking...

The unknown number is x. Three less means subtract 3.

x − 3

Example 4

Write an expression for:

Twice a number plus 8

Thinking...

Twice a number means 2x. Then add 8.

2x + 8
