Collecting Like Terms

Have you ever noticed that we naturally group similar things together? In Algebra we do exactly the same thing.

Learning Objectives

  • Explain what like terms are.
  • Identify like and unlike terms.
  • Simplify algebraic expressions by collecting like terms.
  • Avoid one of the most common Algebra mistakes.

Introduction

This lesson develops Collecting Like Terms from meaning and patterns before moving to calculations. Look for what the quantities, shapes or symbols represent, then connect that idea to the method.

Key Notes

Read the definitions and key facts below carefully. Each rule is most useful when you can explain why it fits the situation, not simply recall it.

🎬 Group only like terms

3x+ 2+ 4x− 1

Only terms with the same variable part can combine.

🤔 Think About This...

Imagine you empty your school bag onto your bed. You have:

  • 3 Maths books
  • 2 English books
  • 4 Maths books
  • 1 English book

Would you leave everything mixed together? Probably not. You would put the Maths books together and the English books together.

Maths Books 3 + 4 = 7

English Books 2 + 1 = 3

That is exactly what collecting like terms means.

📖 What are Like Terms?

Like terms are terms that have exactly the same variable.

For example:

3x     5x

Both have the variable x, so they are like terms.

2a     8a

These are also like terms.

The numbers may be different. The variable must be the same.

❌ Unlike Terms

These cannot be combined.

Example

3x + 5y

Why?

One has x. One has y. Different variables.

Remember: You cannot add apples and oranges. Likewise, you cannot add x's and y's.

📝 Worked Examples

Example 1

Simplify:

3x + 2x

Thinking...

Both terms contain x. Add the numbers.

3x + 2x = 5x

Example 2

8a − 3a

Both terms contain a. Subtract the coefficients.

5a

Example 3

5x + 3 + 2x + 7

Group like terms.

5x + 2x + 3 + 7
7x + 10

🌍 Real-Life Example

Imagine one loaf of bread costs x dollars. You buy:

  • 3 loaves today
  • 2 loaves tomorrow

Total cost:

3x + 2x

Collect like terms.

5x

Common Mistakes
