🎯 Purpose of This Review

In earlier lessons, the heading normally told you which skill to use.

In a test or examination, the question may not tell you whether you need HCF, percentages, a surd, number bases or bounds.

You must identify the mathematical clue and choose the correct method.

This review is about more than calculating.

It is about learning to recognise what kind of problem you are solving.

🤔 Think About This...

🧠

Which method should you use?

Consider these three questions:

  1. Two bells ring every 8 minutes and every 12 minutes. When will they next ring together?
  2. A jacket costing US$80 is reduced by 15%. What is the sale price?
  3. A length is recorded as 7.4 m to one decimal place. What is its error interval?

They are all questions about numbers, but they require three completely different methods.

🧠 StudyNest Method: Approaching a Mixed Question

Step 1: Read the complete question before calculating.

Step 2: Identify the information given.

Step 3: Identify exactly what you are being asked to find.

Step 4: Look for mathematical clues.

Step 5: Choose the correct rule, operation or formula.

Step 6: Show clear working.

Step 7: Check whether your answer is reasonable.

🔴 Section D: Exam-Style Challenge

These questions require several stages or combine more than one Real Numbers skill.

  1. Three traffic lights change every 30 seconds, 45 seconds and 75 seconds.

    They change together at 08:00:00.

    At what time will they next change together?
  2. A school has 168 Mathematics books and 252 Science books. The books are placed into the greatest possible number of identical sets, with no books left over.

    a) How many sets can be formed?
    b) How many Mathematics books are in each set?
    c) How many Science books are in each set?
  3. A shop increases the price of an item by 20% and later reduces the new price by 20%.

    The original price is US$150.

    Find the final price and explain why it is not US$150.
  4. A sum of US$800 is invested at 6% compound interest per year. Find the value after three years.
  5. A square garden has an area of 288 m². Find the exact side length in simplest surd form.
  6. Simplify completely:

    2√75 − √27 + 3√12
  7. Rationalise and simplify:

    12/√3
  8. The terms of a pattern are:

    2, 5, 11, 23, 47, ...

    a) Describe the term-to-term rule.
    b) Find the next two terms.
  9. In Base 5:

    243₅ + 134₅

    Give your answer in Base 5.
  10. A number is written as 24b and has a Base 10 value of 18. Find the base b.
  11. Flour and sugar are mixed in the ratio 5 : 2. A baker uses 1.4 kg of sugar.

    a) Find the mass of flour required.
    b) Find the total mass of the mixture.
  12. A car uses 18 litres of fuel to travel 225 km.

    a) Find the distance travelled per litre.
    b) Estimate how far the car can travel using 30 litres at the same rate.
  13. The distance from Earth to a satellite is 3.84 × 10⁵ km.

    Another satellite is 7.2 × 10⁴ km away.

    Find the difference between the distances in standard form.
  14. A rectangular field has a recorded length of 24 m and a recorded width of 15 m, each correct to the nearest metre.

    Find:
    a) the lower bound for the area;
    b) the upper bound for the area.
  15. A value x is recorded as 6.8 correct to one decimal place.

    Another value y is recorded as 3.2 correct to one decimal place.

    Find:
    a) the lower bound of x + y;
    b) the upper bound of x + y.

✅ Exam-Style Challenge Solutions

Show answers to Questions 61–75
  1. Find the LCM of 30, 45 and 75.

    30 = 2 × 3 × 5
    45 = 3² × 5
    75 = 3 × 5²

    LCM = 2 × 3² × 5²

    = 450 seconds

    450 seconds = 7 minutes 30 seconds

    Next together at 08:07:30.
  2. HCF of 168 and 252 = 84.

    a) 84 sets

    b) 168 ÷ 84 = 2 Mathematics books

    c) 252 ÷ 84 = 3 Science books
  3. Increase:

    150 × 1.20 = 180

    Decrease:

    180 × 0.80 = 144

    Final price = US$144.

    The decrease is calculated from US$180, not from the original US$150.
  4. 800 × 1.06³

    = 952.8128

    = US$952.81
  5. Side = √288

    = √(144 × 2)

    = 12√2 m
  6. 2√75 − √27 + 3√12

    = 2(5√3) − 3√3 + 3(2√3)

    = 10√3 − 3√3 + 6√3

    = 13√3
  7. 12/√3 × √3/√3

    = 12√3/3

    = 4√3
  8. a) Multiply by 2, then add 1.

    b) 47 × 2 + 1 = 95

    95 × 2 + 1 = 191

    Next terms: 95 and 191
  9. 243₅ + 134₅

    = 432₅
  10. 24b = 18

    2b + 4 = 18

    2b = 14

    b = 7
  11. Flour : sugar = 5 : 2

    2 parts = 1.4 kg

    One part = 0.7 kg

    a) Flour = 5 × 0.7 = 3.5 kg

    b) Total = 3.5 + 1.4 = 4.9 kg
  12. a) 225 ÷ 18 = 12.5 km per litre

    b) 12.5 × 30 = 375 km
  13. 3.84 × 10⁵ − 7.2 × 10⁴

    = 3.84 × 10⁵ − 0.72 × 10⁵

    = 3.12 × 10⁵ km
  14. Length:

    23.5 ≤ L < 24.5

    Width:

    14.5 ≤ W < 15.5

    a) Lower area = 23.5 × 14.5

    = 340.75 m²

    b) Upper area = 24.5 × 15.5

    = 379.75 m²
  15. For x:

    6.75 ≤ x < 6.85

    For y:

    3.15 ≤ y < 3.25

    a) Lower bound = 6.75 + 3.15

    = 9.90

    b) Upper bound = 6.85 + 3.25

    = 10.10
