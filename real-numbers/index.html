Why Does This Topic Matter?

Real numbers are the foundation for nearly every branch of mathematics.

🛒

Shopping, budgeting and discounts all depend on working with numbers accurately.

📏

Measurement, scale and estimation rely on understanding values and rounding.

📈

Graphs, equations and formulae all use number operations and notation.

🧮

Strong number sense makes later topics easier to understand.

💡

CENTRAL IDEA

Numbers make sense when we see how they fit together.

Real numbers bring together whole numbers, fractions, decimals, negatives, roots and other values into one connected system.

Ask what kind of number you are working with before choosing a method.
