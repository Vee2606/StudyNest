⚠ Top Mistakes to Avoid

1

Confusing HCF with LCM.

2

Ignoring the correct order of operations.

3

Adding fractions without finding a common denominator.

4

Forgetting to simplify a final fraction or surd.

5

Confusing decimal places with significant figures.

6

Dividing percentage change by the new value instead of the original value.

7

Attempting to combine unlike surds.

8

Using a digit that is not permitted in a number base.

9

Forgetting to convert measurements to the same units.

10

Including the upper bound in an error interval.
