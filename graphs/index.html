Why Does This Topic Matter?

🌡️

Temperature and growth can be tracked over time.

🚗

💰

📊

CENTRAL IDEA

A graph is a picture of a relationship.

Graphs show how one quantity changes as another changes, making patterns and trends visible.

Check the scale and axis labels before reading or drawing a graph.
