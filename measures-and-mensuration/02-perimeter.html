MEASURES & MENSURATION · LESSON 02

Perimeter

Understand boundary length and solve simple and combined-shape problems.

⏱️ 18–22 min 📘 Foundation
r
cm²

🎯 By the End of This Lesson...

  • explain perimeter as total boundary length;
  • calculate the perimeter of common plane shapes;
  • find missing side lengths;
  • calculate perimeters of combined shapes;
  • distinguish perimeter from area.

What Is Perimeter?

Perimeter is the total distance around the outside of a two-dimensional shape.

Key idea: Imagine walking once around the boundary and adding every outside edge.

Useful Perimeter Formulas

Rectangle

P = 2(l + w)

Square

P = 4s

Triangle

P = a + b + c

Regular polygon

P = number of sides × side length

Worked Example: Missing Side

A rectangle has perimeter 34 cm and length 11 cm. Find its width.

1

34 = 2(11 + w).

2

17 = 11 + w.

3

w = 6 cm.

Combined Shapes

For a combined shape, include only the outside boundary. Internal joining edges are not part of the perimeter.

Practice Questions

Foundation
  1. Find the perimeter of a rectangle 9 cm by 4 cm.
  2. Find the perimeter of an equilateral triangle with side 7.5 cm.
  3. A regular hexagon has side 6 cm. Find its perimeter.
Developing
  1. A rectangle has perimeter 50 m and width 9 m. Find the length.
  2. A triangle has sides x, x+2 and x+5. Its perimeter is 31 cm. Find all three sides.
Challenge
  1. An L-shape is formed from a 12 cm by 10 cm rectangle by removing a 5 cm by 4 cm corner. Find the perimeter.

✅ Check Your Work

Show practice answers
  1. 26 cm
  2. 22.5 cm
  3. 36 cm
  4. 50 = 2(l+9), so l = 16 m.
  5. 3x+7=31, so x=8. Sides: 8 cm, 10 cm, 13 cm.
  6. Removing a corner does not change the total perimeter here: 2(12+10)=44 cm.

One-Minute Summary

  • Perimeter measures the outside boundary.
  • Use only outer edges for compound shapes.
  • Perimeter uses linear units such as cm or m.
