Mixed Review

These questions combine conversions, perimeter, area, circles, volume, density, similarity and composite solids.

Part A: Core Skills

  1. Convert 3.6 m² to cm².
  2. Find perimeter of a rectangle 14 cm by 9 cm.
  3. Find area of a triangle with base 18 cm and height 7 cm.
  4. Find circumference of a circle of diameter 12 cm.
  5. Find volume of a cuboid 8 cm by 5 cm by 3 cm.

Part B: Solids and Scale

  1. Find total surface area of a cube side 7 cm.
  2. Find volume of a cylinder radius 4 cm, height 9 cm.
  3. Two similar solids have linear scale factor 3. Smaller volume is 24 cm³. Find larger volume.
  4. A substance has mass 2.4 kg and volume 300 cm³. Find density in g/cm³.

Part C: Application

  1. A circular garden has radius 5 m and is surrounded by a path 2 m wide. Find path area.
  2. A cone of radius 6 cm and height 12 cm is attached to a cylinder of the same radius and height 8 cm. Find total volume.
  3. A 1:100 plan shows a rectangular room 6.5 cm by 4 cm. Find real floor area.

Reflection

  • Can you choose the correct formula without being told?
  • Can you keep units consistent?
  • Can you split composite objects into familiar parts?
  • Can you explain why your answer is reasonable?

Revision Guidance and Readiness
