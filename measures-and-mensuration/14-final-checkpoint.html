🎉 Final Checkpoint

This assessment covers the full Measures & Mensuration topic.

Section A: Foundation

  1. Convert 4.25 km to metres.
  2. Find perimeter of a square side 8.5 cm.
  3. Find area of a trapezium with parallel sides 9 cm and 15 cm, height 6 cm.
  4. Find area of a circle radius 7 cm.
  5. Find volume of a cube side 6 cm.

Section B: Developing

  1. Find surface area of a cuboid 12 cm by 7 cm by 5 cm.
  2. Find capacity in litres of a tank 1.8 m by 1.2 m by 0.75 m.
  3. Find total surface area of a closed cylinder radius 3 cm, height 10 cm.
  4. A cone has volume 150π cm³ and radius 5 cm. Find height.
  5. A block has density 2.5 g/cm³ and volume 160 cm³. Find mass.

Section C: Exam Style

  1. A rectangle 20 m by 14 m contains a circular pond radius 4 m. Find remaining area.
  2. Two similar solids have surface areas 36 cm² and 225 cm². Find linear scale factor and volume ratio.
  3. A hollow cylinder has outer radius 8 cm, inner radius 5 cm and height 20 cm. Find volume of material.
  4. A cone of radius 9 cm and height 15 cm has a similar cone of radius 3 cm removed. Find frustum volume.
