MEASURES & MENSURATION · LESSON 03

Area of Plane Shapes

Calculate and compare the areas of common and combined two-dimensional shapes.

⏱️ 20–24 min 📘 Foundation
r
cm²

🎯 By the End of This Lesson...

  • calculate areas of rectangles, triangles, parallelograms and trapezia;
  • identify perpendicular height;
  • find areas of combined shapes;
  • calculate missing dimensions from area;
  • use square units correctly.

What Is Area?

Area measures how much flat surface a shape covers.

Key idea: Area counts square units inside a boundary.

Rectangle and Triangle

Rectangle

A = length × width

Triangle

A = ½ × base × perpendicular height

Parallelogram and Trapezium

Parallelogram

A = base × perpendicular height

Trapezium

A = ½(a+b)h, where a and b are parallel sides

Worked Example: Trapezium

Parallel sides are 8 cm and 14 cm, height is 5 cm.

1

A = ½(8+14)×5.

2

A = ½×22×5 = 55 cm².

Combined Shapes

Split a complex figure into familiar shapes, find each area, then add or subtract.

Practice Questions

Foundation
  1. Find the area of a rectangle 12 cm by 7 cm.
  2. Find the area of a triangle with base 16 cm and height 9 cm.
  3. Find the area of a parallelogram with base 11 m and perpendicular height 6 m.
Developing
  1. A trapezium has parallel sides 9 cm and 15 cm and height 8 cm. Find its area.
  2. A rectangle has area 96 cm² and width 8 cm. Find its length.
  3. A shape consists of a 10 m by 6 m rectangle and a triangle of base 10 m and height 4 m. Find total area.
Challenge
  1. A rectangular garden 18 m by 12 m contains a rectangular pond 5 m by 4 m. Find the area of land outside the pond.
