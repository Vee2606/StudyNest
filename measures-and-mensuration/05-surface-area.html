Surface Area

Understand exposed faces, nets and total outside area of solids.

⏱️ 20–25 min 📘 Developing
r
cm²

🎯 By the End of This Lesson...

  • explain surface area as the total outside area of a solid;
  • identify faces of cubes, cuboids, prisms and cylinders;
  • use nets to understand surface area;
  • calculate total surface area;
  • solve missing-dimension problems.

What Is Surface Area?

Surface area is the total area of every exposed face of a three-dimensional object.

Key idea: Imagine unfolding the solid into a flat net and adding the areas of all pieces.

Net of a Cuboid

Useful Formulas

Cube: SA = 6s²
Cuboid: SA = 2(lw + lh + wh)

Worked Example: Cuboid

Find the surface area of a cuboid 8 cm by 5 cm by 3 cm.

1

lw = 8×5 = 40.

2

lh = 8×3 = 24.

3

wh = 5×3 = 15.

4

SA = 2(40+24+15)=158 cm².

Open Containers

If a box has no lid, do not include the missing top face.

Surface area questions depend on which faces are exposed. Read words such as open, closed, lidless and hollow carefully.

Practice Questions

Foundation
  1. Find the surface area of a cube with side 6 cm.
  2. Find the surface area of a cuboid 10 cm by 4 cm by 3 cm.
  3. A cube has surface area 150 cm². Find its side length.
Developing
  1. Find the area of cardboard needed for an open box 12 cm by 8 cm by 5 cm.
  2. A cuboid has dimensions 9 cm by 6 cm by h cm and surface area 300 cm². Find h.
Challenge
  1. Two cubes of side 4 cm are joined face-to-face. Find the surface area of the new solid.

✅ Check Your Work

Show practice answers
  1. 6×36=216 cm².
  2. 2(40+30+12)=164 cm².
  3. 6s²=150, so s²=25 and s=5 cm.
  4. Base 96 + two 12×5 faces + two 8×5 faces = 296 cm².
  5. 2(54+9h+6h)=300 → 108+30h=300 → h=6.4 cm.
  6. Two separate cubes have 192 cm², but the two joined faces become internal: 192−32=160 cm².

One-Minute Summary

  • Surface area is the total exposed outer area.
  • Nets help reveal all faces.
  • Missing or joined faces must not be counted.
