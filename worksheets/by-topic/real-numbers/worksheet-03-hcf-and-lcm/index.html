Common mistakes to remember

  • A factor divides a number exactly; a multiple is produced by multiplying.
  • 1 is neither prime nor composite.
  • Prime factorisation must contain prime factors only.
  • Common factors divide both numbers; common multiples are divisible by both numbers.
  • When counting factors from prime powers, add 1 to each exponent and multiply.
