Common mistakes to remember

  • Zero is whole, integer, rational and real, but it is not natural under the StudyNest convention.
  • Negative integers are not whole numbers.
  • Recurring decimals are rational because they can be written as fractions.
  • Not every square root is irrational: simplify it first.
  • A number can belong to several number families at the same time.
