GEOMETRY · LESSON 14

Symmetry

Identify line and rotational symmetry in shapes.

⏱️ 22–30 min 📘 Developing
90° 180° θ

🎯 By the End of This Lesson...

  • identify line symmetry;
  • identify rotational symmetry;
  • state order of rotational symmetry;
  • complete symmetric figures;
  • recognise symmetry in regular polygons.

Line Symmetry

A line of symmetry divides a figure into two mirror-image halves.

Rotational Symmetry

A figure has rotational symmetry if it matches itself during a turn of less than 360°.

Order of rotational symmetry = number of matching positions in one full turn.

Common Examples

Square

4 lines; rotational order 4.

Rectangle

2 lines; rotational order 2.

Equilateral triangle

3 lines; rotational order 3.

Regular pentagon

5 lines; rotational order 5.

Try It: Rotational Symmetry

For a regular polygon, the order of rotational symmetry equals the number of sides.

Practice Questions

  1. How many lines of symmetry does a square have?
  2. State the rotational order of a rectangle.
  3. How many lines of symmetry does a regular hexagon have?
  4. Does a scalene triangle have line symmetry?
  5. State the rotational order of a regular octagon.

One-Minute Summary

  • Line symmetry creates mirror halves.
  • Rotational order counts matching positions in 360°.
