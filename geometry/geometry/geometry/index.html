What Will You Learn?

Why Does Geometry Matter?

Geometry is used whenever people design, measure, build or describe space.

Architects use it when designing buildings. Engineers use it when planning structures. Tailors use it when creating clothing patterns. Artists and software designers use it to build balanced and attractive images.

Geometry helps us understand not only what shapes look like, but also how and why their properties work.

Geometry Is a Visual Language

Do not worry when a diagram looks complicated at first.

Begin by identifying what you know: points, line markings, equal sides, parallel arrows and angle symbols. Then work through the information one step at a time.

You do not need to see the whole solution immediately. Begin with one fact, then use it to discover the next.
