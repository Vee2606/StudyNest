GEOMETRY · LESSON 10

Constructions and Loci

Use ruler and compasses to create accurate geometric constructions.

⏱️ 22–30 min 📘 Developing
90° 180° θ

🎯 By the End of This Lesson...

  • construct perpendicular and angle bisectors;
  • construct 60°, 90° and common derived angles;
  • construct triangles accurately;
  • understand basic loci;
  • use ruler and compasses correctly.

Construction Rules

Keep compass arcs visible. They are part of the mathematical evidence.

Perpendicular Bisector

  1. Open compasses to more than half the segment length.
  2. Draw arcs above and below from each endpoint.
  3. Join the arc intersections.

Angle Bisector

  1. Draw an arc from the vertex crossing both arms.
  2. From the two crossing points, draw equal arcs that meet.
  3. Join the vertex to the new intersection.

Practice Questions

  3. Which locus contains points 4 cm from point P?
  4. What angle is formed when a perpendicular is constructed?

One-Minute Summary

  • Use ruler and compasses, not estimation.
  • Bisectors create equal parts.
  • Loci describe sets of positions satisfying a rule.
