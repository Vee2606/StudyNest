GEOMETRY · LESSON 11

Bearings

Measure and solve compass and three-figure bearing problems.

⏱️ 22–30 min 📘 Developing
90° 180° θ

🎯 By the End of This Lesson...

  • understand compass and three-figure bearings;
  • measure bearings clockwise from north;
  • draw accurate bearing diagrams;
  • calculate reverse bearings;
  • solve map-style bearing problems.

Three-Figure Bearings

Bearings are measured clockwise from north and written using three digits.

East

090°

South

180°

West

270°

North

000° or 360°

Reverse Bearings

Add or subtract 180°.

Bearing of B from A is 065°.

Bearing of A from B is 245°.

Try It: Rotate a Three-Figure Bearing

Practice Questions

  1. Write the bearing of east.
  2. Write 35° clockwise from north as a three-figure bearing.
  3. Find the reverse bearing of 120°.
  4. Find the reverse bearing of 305°.
  5. A ship travels on bearing 070°. Describe its general direction.

One-Minute Summary

  • Measure clockwise from north.
  • Use three digits.
  • Reverse bearings differ by 180°.
