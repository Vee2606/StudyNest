GEOMETRY · LESSON 02

Angles on Straight Lines and Around a Point

Use 180° and 360° angle rules confidently.

⏱️ 18–24 min 📘 Foundation
90° 180° θ

🎯 By the End of This Lesson...

  • use angles on a straight line to total 180°;
  • use angles around a point to total 360°;
  • solve missing-angle equations;
  • identify complementary and supplementary angles;
  • explain each calculation using an angle rule.

Angles on a Straight Line

Angles on a straight line add up to 180°.

Angles Around a Point

Angles around a point add up to 360°.

Three angles around a point are 85°, 120° and x.

x = 360° − 85° − 120° = 155°.

Complementary and Supplementary

Complementary

Two angles that add to 90°.

Supplementary

Two angles that add to 180°.

Algebra in Angle Problems

Adjacent angles on a straight line are (3x+10)° and (2x−5)°.

(3x+10)+(2x−5)=180

5x+5=180, so x=35.

The angles are 115° and 65°.

Practice Questions

Foundation
  1. Find x if x+47°=180°.
  2. Angles around a point are 90°, 75°, 110° and x. Find x.
  3. Find the complement of 38°.
Developing
  1. Two supplementary angles are in the ratio 2:3. Find them.
  2. Angles on a straight line are (4x+8)° and (2x+16)°. Find x and both angles.
Challenge
  1. Four angles around a point are x, 2x, 3x and 4x. Find all four angles.

One-Minute Summary

  • Straight line: 180°.
  • Around a point: 360°.
  • Complementary: 90°.
  • Supplementary: 180°.
