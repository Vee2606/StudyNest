Why Does This Topic Matter?

Geometry helps us describe the world around us with precision.

🏗️

Builders and designers rely on accurate angles and measurements.

🧭

Maps, bearings and directions depend on geometric reasoning.

🪞

Reflection, symmetry and shape properties appear in art and design.

📏

Many practical problems become easier when the diagram is understood.

💡

CENTRAL IDEA

Geometry is about seeing structure.

When we understand the relationships between points, lines and shapes, we can reason clearly and solve problems with confidence.

Start by reading the diagram carefully before choosing a rule.
