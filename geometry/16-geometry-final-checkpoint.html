Section A: Core Knowledge

  1. Classify 310°.
  2. Find the supplement of 74°.
  3. Find the vertically opposite angle to 126°.
  4. Find a co-interior partner of 58°.
  5. State the angle sum of a triangle.

Section B: Polygons and Circles

  1. Find the interior-angle sum of a 9-sided polygon.
  2. Find one interior angle of a regular hexagon.
  3. A circle has diameter 26 cm. Find its radius.
  4. An angle at the centre is 136°. Find the matching circumference angle.
  5. One angle of a cyclic quadrilateral is 95°. Find the opposite angle.

Section C: Applications

  1. Find the reverse bearing of 214°.
  2. An angle of depression is 27°. Find the corresponding angle of elevation.
  3. Similar figures have scale factor 4. A side of 6.5 cm is enlarged. Find the new length.
  4. Name the congruence test using right angle, hypotenuse and one side.
  5. State the number of lines of symmetry and rotational order of a square.
