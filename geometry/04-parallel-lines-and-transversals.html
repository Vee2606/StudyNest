GEOMETRY · LESSON 04

Parallel Lines and Transversals

Use corresponding, alternate and co-interior angle rules.

⏱️ 20–26 min 📘 Developing
90° 180° θ

Learning Objectives

  • identify corresponding angles;
  • identify alternate angles;
  • identify co-interior angles;
  • calculate missing angles on parallel lines;
  • recognise when parallel-line rules may be used.

Introduction

A transversal is a line that crosses two or more other lines.

When the crossed lines are parallel, the angles repeat in a predictable pattern. Understanding the positions comes before choosing a rule.

Key Notes

Corresponding

Same relative position. They are equal.

Alternate

Inside the parallel lines on opposite sides of the transversal. They are equal.

Co-interior / Allied

Inside the parallel lines on the same side. They add to 180°.

Applying the Relationships

One angle formed by a transversal is 68°.

Its corresponding angle is 68°.

Its alternate angle is 68°.

The co-interior angle is 180°−68°=112°.

Recognition Guide

  • Corresponding angles often form an F shape.
  • Alternate angles often form a Z shape.
  • Co-interior angles often form a C or U shape.
These rules require parallel lines. Look for arrow markings or a statement that the lines are parallel.

Try It: Parallel-Line Angle Families

🧠 Let’s Think Together

Two parallel lines are crossed by a transversal. One acute angle is 68°. Equal-angle relationships copy 68° to the matching corresponding and alternate positions. Every neighbouring obtuse angle is 180° − 68° = 112°.

The diagram therefore contains only two angle sizes: 68° and 112°.

Worked Examples

Example 1: Corresponding angles

A corresponding angle to 74° is 74° because corresponding angles between parallel lines are equal.

Example 2: Co-interior angles

If one co-interior angle is 63°, its partner is 180° − 63° = 117°.

Example 3: Algebra

Corresponding angles are (3x + 10)° and (5x − 30)°. They are equal, so 3x + 10 = 5x − 30 and x = 20.

Practice Questions

Foundation
  1. If a corresponding angle is 73°, find its matching angle.
  2. If an alternate angle is 118°, find the other alternate angle.
  3. A co-interior angle is 64°. Find its partner.
Developing
  1. Corresponding angles are (3x+10)° and (5x−30)°. Find x.
  2. Co-interior angles are (2x+20)° and (4x−8)°. Find x.
Challenge
  1. An acute angle made by a transversal is 38°. List all eight angles formed at the two intersections.

✅ Check Your Work

Show practice answers
  1. 73°.
  2. 118°.
  3. 116°.
  4. 3x+10=5x−30, so x=20.
  5. 6x+12=180, so x=28.
  6. Four angles are 38° and four are 142°.

Common Mistakes

  • Using parallel-line rules when the lines are not marked or stated to be parallel.
  • Confusing alternate angles with co-interior angles.
  • Saying co-interior angles are equal instead of checking that they total 180°.

Exam Focus

Name the relationship used in every step. Look first for parallel-line arrow markings, then identify the angle positions before calculating.

Summary

  • Corresponding angles are equal.
  • Alternate angles are equal.
  • Co-interior angles total 180°.
  • Use these rules only when the lines are parallel.
