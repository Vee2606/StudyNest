TRIGONOMETRY · LESSON 09

The Cosine Rule

Solve triangles using SAS or SSS information.

⏱️ 25–35 min 📘 Challenge 📐 Diagrams included
sin cos tan

🎯 Learning Objectives

  • recognise when the cosine rule applies;
  • find a side from two sides and included angle;
  • find an angle from three sides;
  • label sides opposite their matching angles.

The Cosine Rule

a²=b²+c²−2bc cos A

Use it for SAS (two sides and included angle) or SSS (three sides).

Worked Example: Find a Side

b=7 cm, c=10 cm and A=60°.

a²=7²+10²−2(7)(10)cos60°

a²=49+100−70=79

a=√79≈8.89 cm

Worked Example: Find an Angle

a=8, b=6, c=5.

cosA=(b²+c²−a²)/(2bc)

cosA=(36+25−64)/(60)=−0.05

A=cos⁻¹(−0.05)

A≈92.9°

Practice

  1. b=8, c=11, A=50°. Find a.
  2. a=9, b=7, c=6. Find A.
  3. Why can cosine be negative when finding an obtuse angle?

📚 Lesson Wrap-Up

📘 Remember

  • Label the triangle before choosing a formula.
  • Use degree mode unless the question says otherwise.
  • Keep full calculator values until the final rounding step.

⚠️ Common Mistakes

  • Using the wrong reference angle.
  • Rounding too early.
  • Forgetting units or the degree symbol.

⭐ Exam Tips

  • Write the formula before substituting.
  • Sketch and label any diagram that is not already provided.
  • Check whether the answer is sensible for the shape.
