TRIGONOMETRY · LESSON 08

The Sine Rule

Solve non-right triangles using opposite side-angle pairs.

⏱️ 25–35 min 📘 Challenge 📐 Diagrams included
sin cos tan

🎯 Learning Objectives

  • recognise when the sine rule applies;
  • match sides with opposite angles;
  • find missing sides and angles;
  • handle the ambiguous case cautiously.

The Sine Rule

a/sin A = b/sin B = c/sin C

When to Use It

Use the sine rule when you know an opposite side-angle pair and at least one other side or angle.

Worked Example

A=42°, a=9 cm and B=68°. Find b.

b/sin68°=9/sin42°

b=9sin68°/sin42°

b≈12.5 cm

Practice

  1. A=35°, a=8 cm, B=74°. Find b.
  2. A=48°, a=12 cm, b=9 cm. Find B.
  3. What essential matching relationship must be identified first?

✅ Check Your Work

Show answers and working
  1. b=8sin74°/sin35°≈13.4 cm.
  2. sinB=9sin48°/12≈0.5574, so B≈33.9°. A second possible angle must be checked against the triangle sum and context.
  3. Each side must be paired with its opposite angle.

📚 Lesson Wrap-Up

📘 Remember

  • Label the triangle before choosing a formula.
  • Use degree mode unless the question says otherwise.
  • Keep full calculator values until the final rounding step.

⚠️ Common Mistakes

  • Using the wrong reference angle.
  • Rounding too early.
  • Forgetting units or the degree symbol.

⭐ Exam Tips

  • Write the formula before substituting.
  • Sketch and label any diagram that is not already provided.
  • Check whether the answer is sensible for the shape.
