TRIGONOMETRY · LESSON 02

SOH CAH TOA

Choose sine, cosine or tangent from the information in the triangle.

⏱️ 25–35 min 📘 Foundation 📐 Diagrams included
sin cos tan

🎯 Learning Objectives

  • state sine, cosine and tangent ratios;
  • connect each ratio to the correct side pair;
  • select a ratio from known and unknown information;
  • avoid memorising without understanding.

SOH CAH TOA

SOH

sin θ = Opposite ÷ Hypotenuse

CAH

cos θ = Adjacent ÷ Hypotenuse

TOA

tan θ = Opposite ÷ Adjacent

How to Choose the Ratio

Circle the side you know and the side you need.
↓
Which ratio contains exactly those two side names?
↓
Opposite + Hypotenuse → sine
Adjacent + Hypotenuse → cosine
Opposite + Adjacent → tangent

Practice

  1. Which ratio uses opposite and hypotenuse?
  2. Which ratio uses adjacent and hypotenuse?
  3. Which ratio uses opposite and adjacent?
  4. You know the hypotenuse and need the adjacent side. Choose the ratio.
  5. You know the opposite and need the adjacent side. Choose the ratio.

✅ Check Your Work

Show answers and working
  1. Sine.
  2. Cosine.
  3. Tangent.
  4. Cosine, because it contains adjacent and hypotenuse.
  5. Tangent, because it contains opposite and adjacent.

📚 Lesson Wrap-Up

📘 Remember

  • Label the triangle before choosing a formula.
  • Use degree mode unless the question says otherwise.
  • Keep full calculator values until the final rounding step.

⚠️ Common Mistakes

  • Using the wrong reference angle.
  • Rounding too early.
  • Forgetting units or the degree symbol.

⭐ Exam Tips

  • Write the formula before substituting.
  • Sketch and label any diagram that is not already provided.
  • Check whether the answer is sensible for the shape.
