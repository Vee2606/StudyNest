🎉 Money & Finance Final Checkpoint

This checkpoint brings together the complete topic. Work without opening previous notes, then check your reasoning carefully.

Confidence Checklist

  • I can prepare and interpret a budget.
  • I can solve percentage and reverse-percentage problems.
  • I can calculate profit, VAT, wages and commission.
  • I can calculate simple and compound interest.
  • I can compare hire-purchase plans.
  • I can use exchange rates and financial documents.

Section A: Foundation

  1. A budget has income US$420 and expenses US$368. Find the balance.
  2. Decrease US$600 by 18%.
  3. An item costs US$80 and sells for US$104. Find profit percentage.
  4. Find net pay if gross pay is US$720 and deductions are US$86.
  5. Find simple interest on US$500 at 7% for 4 years.

Section B: Developing

  1. A US$450 item receives 20% discount, then 15% VAT. Find final price.
  2. A salesperson earns US$300 basic plus 4.5% commission on US$8,000 sales. Find gross pay.
  3. Find the compound amount on US$2,400 at 8% for 3 years.
  4. Cash price US$950; deposit US$190; 24 instalments of US$38. Find total HP cost and extra percentage.
  5. At US$1=R18.25, convert R10,950 to US dollars.

Section C: Exam Style

  1. After a 12% discount and 15% VAT, an appliance costs US$404.80. Find the original marked price.
  2. A worker earns US$5.80 per hour for 40 hours, time-and-a-half for 10 hours and double time for 4 hours. Find gross pay.
  3. An investment grows to US$3,993 after 3 years at 10% compound interest. Find the principal.
  4. A bank statement opens at US$1,240, receives US$460 and has debits of US$385.50, US$210.75, US$96 and US$12.25. Find closing balance.
