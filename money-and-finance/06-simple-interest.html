🌍 Where Will You Use This?

Interest appears when money is saved, invested or borrowed. Simple interest is calculated only on the original amount.

The Meaning of Interest

Principal P Original amount
Rate r Percentage per time period
Time t Length of investment or loan

Simple Interest Formula

I = Prt / 100
Amount = Principal + Interest
Simple interest is always calculated on the original principal.

Worked Example 1

Find simple interest on US$600 at 8% per year for 3 years.

1

P=600, r=8, t=3.

2

I=600×8×3÷100 = US$144.

3

Amount = 600+144 = US$744.

Matching Time Units

If the rate is per year, express time in years.

US$900 at 10% per year for 9 months.

1

9 months = 9/12 = 0.75 years.

2

I=900×10×0.75÷100 = US$67.50.

Finding a Missing Value

Interest is US$120 on US$500 for 4 years. Find the rate.

1

120=500×r×4÷100.

2

120=20r.

3

r=6% per year.

💼 Money Smart Tip

Always check whether the question asks for interest only or the total amount including the principal.

Practice Questions

Foundation
  1. Find simple interest on US$400 at 5% for 3 years.
  2. Find the total amount for US$750 at 6% for 2 years.
  3. Find interest on US$1,200 at 9% for 8 months.
Developing
  1. US$180 interest is earned on US$900 for 5 years. Find the rate.
  2. At 7% simple interest, how long will US$500 take to earn US$105?
  3. A loan grows to US$1,260 after 4 years at 6.5% simple interest. Find the principal.
Challenge
  1. Two investments earn the same simple interest. The first is US$800 at 9% for 2 years. The second is invested at 6% for 4 years. Find the second principal.

One-Minute Summary

  • Simple interest uses only the original principal.
  • I=Prt/100.
  • Amount = principal + interest.
  • Time units must match the rate period.
