🌍 Where Will You Use This?

Financial documents help people and businesses track money, prove transactions and check what is owed or paid.

Bank Statement Language

Credit

Money entering the account.

Debit

Money leaving the account.

Running Balance

Opening balanceUS$420.00
Salary credit+US$350.00
Rent payment−US$180.00
Bank charge−US$6.50
Closing balanceUS$583.50

Invoices

An invoice requests payment and usually shows quantities, unit prices, discounts, VAT and the amount due.

12 notebooks at US$2.50 each, less 10% discount, plus 15% VAT.

1

Subtotal=12×2.50=US$30.

2

After discount=30×0.90=US$27.

3

Total with VAT=27×1.15=US$31.05.

Receipts

A receipt confirms payment has been made. Check item totals, discounts, tax, amount tendered and change.

Checking for Errors

  1. Recalculate quantity × unit price.
  2. Check the order of discount and VAT.
  3. Confirm credits were added and debits subtracted.
  4. Recalculate the final balance.

Practice Questions

Foundation
  1. An account opens with US$260, receives US$90 and pays US$75. Find the balance.
  2. A bank charge of US$4.50 is deducted from US$128. Find the new balance.
  3. Find the subtotal for 15 items at US$3.20 each.
Developing
  1. Opening balance US$500; deposit US$225; payments US$84.50, US$120 and bank charge US$7.25. Find closing balance.
  2. An invoice lists 8 items at US$12.50 each, 5% discount and 15% VAT. Find amount due.
  3. A customer pays US$100 for goods costing US$76.80. Find the change.
Challenge
  1. A statement balance should be US$642.30, but it shows US$616.80. One deposit was omitted. Find the omitted deposit.

One-Minute Summary

  • Credits add to an account; debits reduce it.
  • A running balance updates after every transaction.
  • Invoices request payment; receipts confirm payment.
  • Always verify quantities, percentages and totals.
