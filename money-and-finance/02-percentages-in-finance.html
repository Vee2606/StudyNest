🌍 Where Will You Use This?

Percentages appear in discounts, interest, tax, commission, inflation, profit, exam marks and salary increases.

What Does Percent Mean?

Percent means “out of 100”.

25% = 25/100 = 0.25
Convert a percentage to a decimal by dividing by 100.

Finding a Percentage of a Quantity

Percentage amount = percentage ÷ 100 × whole amount

Find 18% of US$250.

1

18% = 0.18.

2

0.18 × 250 = 45.

3

18% of US$250 is US$45.

Percentage Increase and Decrease

Increase by r% → multiply by 1 + r/100
Decrease by r% → multiply by 1 − r/100

Increase US$320 by 12%.

Multiplier = 1.12, so new amount = 320 × 1.12 = US$358.40.

Decrease US$480 by 15%.

Multiplier = 0.85, so new amount = 480 × 0.85 = US$408.

Finding Percentage Change

Percentage change = change ÷ original amount × 100%
Always divide by the original amount, not the new amount.

Reverse Percentages

A reverse-percentage question gives the value after a change and asks for the original.

After a 20% discount, a bag costs US$64. Find the original price.

1

After 20% off, 80% remains.

2

0.80 × original = 64.

3

Original = 64 ÷ 0.80 = US$80.

Successive Percentage Changes

Two percentage changes act on different amounts, so they should be applied one after another.

A price rises by 10%, then falls by 10%.

Overall multiplier = 1.10 × 0.90 = 0.99.

The final price is 99% of the original, so it has fallen by 1%.

💼 Money Smart Tip

A 20% increase followed by a 20% decrease does not return to the original value because the second percentage is applied to a different amount.

🔍 How Do I Recognise the Method?

  • “of” → multiply.
  • “increase by” → use a multiplier above 1.
  • “decrease by” → use a multiplier below 1.
  • “original price” after a change → reverse percentage.
  • “percentage change” → change ÷ original × 100%.

Practice Questions

Foundation
  1. Find 12% of US$350.
  2. Increase US$240 by 15%.
  3. Decrease US$560 by 22%.
  4. A price rises from US$80 to US$92. Find the percentage increase.
Developing
  1. After a 30% discount, a phone costs US$245. Find the original price.
  2. A salary increases by 8% to US$540. Find the original salary.
  3. A price increases by 20% and then decreases by 15%. Find the overall percentage change.
Challenge
  1. A shop reduces a price by 10% and then by a further 20%. The final price is US$216. Find the original price and the single equivalent discount percentage.

One-Minute Summary

  • Percent means out of 100.
  • Use multipliers for increases and decreases.
  • Percentage change uses the original amount as denominator.
  • Reverse percentages undo the multiplier.
  • Successive changes must be applied one at a time.
