Hire Purchase and Instalments

Compare cash prices, deposits, instalments and the true total cost of buying over time.

🎯 By the End of This Lesson...

  • identify cash price, deposit and instalments;
  • calculate total hire-purchase cost;
  • calculate the extra amount paid above cash price;
  • compare payment plans;
  • solve reverse hire-purchase problems.

🌍 Where Will You Use This?

Hire purchase is used when an item is taken immediately but paid for over time through a deposit and instalments.

The Structure of Hire Purchase

Deposit Amount paid first
Instalments Regular payments
Total HP Cost Deposit + all instalments
Total HP Cost = Deposit + (Number of Instalments × Instalment Amount)

Worked Example 1

A television has a cash price of US$600. Under hire purchase, a deposit of US$120 is paid followed by 18 monthly instalments of US$32.

1

Total instalments = 18×32 = US$576.

2

Total HP cost = 120+576 = US$696.

3

Extra paid = 696−600 = US$96.

Percentage Extra Paid

Extra percentage = Extra paid ÷ Cash price × 100%

Using the television example:

Extra percentage = 96÷600×100 = 16%.

Reverse Problem

Total HP cost is US$1,040. The deposit is US$200 and there are 24 equal instalments. Find each instalment.

1

Total instalments = 1040−200 = US$840.

2

Each instalment = 840÷24 = US$35.

Comparing Two Plans

Plan A: US$100 deposit + 12 payments of US$48.
Plan B: US$160 deposit + 10 payments of US$50.

Plan A total = 100+12×48 = US$676.

Plan B total = 160+10×50 = US$660.

Plan B is cheaper by US$16.

💼 Money Smart Tip

A lower monthly instalment does not always mean a cheaper plan. Compare the total amount paid.

Practice Questions

Foundation
  1. A fridge requires US$150 deposit and 20 payments of US$28. Find the total HP cost.
  2. The cash price is US$620 and HP total is US$744. Find the extra paid.
  3. A deposit of US$90 and 15 instalments produce a total cost of US$540. Find each instalment.
Developing
  1. A laptop cash price is US$800. HP terms are 25% deposit and 18 payments of US$39. Find total HP cost and extra percentage.
  2. Compare:
    1. US$120 deposit + 24×US$30;
    2. US$200 deposit + 18×US$36.
Challenge
  1. A cash price is US$900. Under HP, a 20% deposit is followed by equal monthly instalments for 2 years. The customer pays 18% more than the cash price in total. Find each monthly instalment.

One-Minute Summary

  • Total HP cost = deposit + all instalments.
  • Extra paid = HP total − cash price.
  • Compare total costs, not only monthly payments.
  • Reverse problems often require subtracting the deposit first.
