Mixed Review: Choose the Method

These questions combine budgeting, percentages, profit, wages, interest, hire purchase, exchange rates and financial documents.

Part A: Core Skills

  1. Increase US$480 by 12%.
  2. Find the original price if US$168 is the price after a 30% discount.
  3. An item costs US$90 and sells for US$117. Find profit percentage.
  4. Find 15% VAT on US$260.
  5. Find 5% commission on US$7,200.

Part B: Interest and Payment Plans

  1. Find simple interest on US$900 at 8% for 2.5 years.
  2. Find the amount on US$1,500 at 6% compound interest for 3 years.
  3. A cash price is US$700. HP terms are US$140 deposit and 20×US$34. Find extra paid.

Part C: Applications

  1. At US$1=R18.60, convert R7,440 to US dollars.
  2. A worker earns US$6 per hour for 40 hours and time-and-a-half for 8 overtime hours. Find gross pay.
  3. An invoice has a subtotal of US$360, a 12% discount and 15% VAT. Find the final amount.
  4. An account opens at US$750, receives US$320 and has debits of US$165.50, US$210 and US$8.75. Find closing balance.

Reflection

  • Can you choose the correct percentage multiplier?
  • Can you distinguish simple from compound interest?
  • Can you compare cash and instalment plans?
  • Can you interpret rates and financial documents?
