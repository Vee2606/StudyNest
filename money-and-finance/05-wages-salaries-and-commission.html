🌍 Where Will You Use This?

Payslips, employment contracts and sales jobs use wages, salaries, overtime, commission, bonuses and deductions.

Wages and Salaries

Wage

Often based on hours worked, days worked or units produced.

Salary

A regular fixed payment, usually monthly or annually.

Basic Pay

Pay = Rate × Time worked

A worker earns US$4.50 per hour for 36 hours.

Pay = 4.50 × 36 = US$162.

Overtime

Overtime may be paid at time-and-a-half, double time or another stated rate.

A worker earns US$6 per hour and works 40 normal hours plus 8 hours at time-and-a-half.

1

Normal pay = 40×6 = US$240.

2

Overtime rate = 1.5×6 = US$9.

3

Overtime pay = 8×9 = US$72.

4

Total gross pay = US$312.

Commission

Commission = Commission rate × Sales value

A salesperson earns 6% commission on US$4,500 sales.

Commission = 0.06×4500 = US$270.

Gross and Net Pay

Gross pay = Basic pay + Overtime + Commission + Bonuses
Net pay = Gross pay − Deductions

💼 Money Smart Tip

Gross pay is not the amount taken home. Always check deductions to understand net pay.

Practice Questions

Foundation
  1. Find the wage for 32 hours at US$5.25 per hour.
  2. Find 4% commission on US$8,000 sales.
  3. Gross pay is US$640 and deductions are US$93. Find net pay.
Developing
  1. A worker earns US$7 per hour for 40 hours and double time for 5 overtime hours. Find gross pay.
  2. A salesperson earns a basic salary of US$350 plus 5% commission on US$6,400 sales. Find gross pay.
  3. A payslip shows gross pay of US$780. Deductions are 8% of gross pay plus a fixed US$25. Find net pay.
Challenge
  1. A worker earns US$5.50 per hour for the first 40 hours, time-and-a-half for the next 10 hours and double time beyond 50 hours. Find the gross pay for 56 hours.

One-Minute Summary

  • Wages often depend on time or output.
  • Overtime uses an increased hourly rate.
  • Commission is a percentage of sales.
  • Net pay is gross pay minus deductions.
