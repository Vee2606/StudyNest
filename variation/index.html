Why Does This Topic Matter?

Variation appears whenever two quantities are connected.

🛒

The more items you buy, the more you usually pay.

🚗

The faster you travel, the less time the journey takes.

🎂

Doubling a recipe doubles every ingredient.

⚖️

The cost of fruit may depend on its weight.

💡

CENTRAL IDEA

Quantities can change together in predictable ways.

Variation describes the relationship between quantities. Once the relationship is understood, one value can be used to calculate another.

Understand what is changing before choosing a formula.
